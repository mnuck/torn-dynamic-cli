The cheapest points on the board

Staying out of reach costs nothing and takes no energy. Hospitalise before logging off, travel, or sit in jail — an unattended member is free score for the other faction. It's the one improvement available to everyone regardless of stats.

The ceiling above is not a target: you can't always get to safety before the first hit lands, and people get caught mid-action. Treat it as the size of the opportunity, not points we should have had.
